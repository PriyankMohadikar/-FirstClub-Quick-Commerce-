# FirstClub - Quick Commerce Sales & Operations Dashboard

FirstClub is a quick-commerce concept built around getting everyday essentials to customers in around 20–30 minutes. For this project, I used the provided order, delivery, customer, product, and marketing data to understand how the business is performing and where it could improve.

I built the dashboard in **Microsoft Excel**, using PivotTables, charts, and slicers to make the data easier to explore. The goal was to bring sales, delivery operations, customer activity, and campaign performance into one place instead of looking at each area separately.

## What’s included

The workbook covers several parts of the business:

- **Sales trends:** Explore sales patterns over time and identify periods of higher activity.
- **Delivery performance:** Review on-time delivery, delivery-time buckets, and delivery speed across partners.
- **Peak-hour analysis:** See when order demand is highest.
- **Customer and product analysis:** Compare customer segments and top-selling product categories.
- **Marketing performance:** Review channel impressions, click/conversion rates, and campaign ROAS.
- **Partner performance:** Compare order volumes with average delivery times.

## What I learned from the analysis

- Delivery performance is worth tracking alongside order volume; a busy partner is not automatically the fastest partner.
- Looking at delivery-time buckets helps show how many orders fall into each delivery window and where delays may be concentrated.
- Peak-hour patterns can help with planning staffing and delivery capacity.
- Comparing impressions, clicks, conversions, and ROAS gives a more useful view of campaign performance than impressions alone.
- Bringing sales, operations, customers, and marketing into one dashboard makes it easier to spot trade-offs and decide what to investigate next.
